Server Rules

  • No Griefing Spawn/Safezones: Engaging in combat or destruction in designated safe areas is strictly prohibited.
  • Factions & War: Wars must be properly declared through the server's formal system before structural destruction of another nation occurs.
  • Physics Limits: Excessively large physics objects designed purely to lag the server or crash clients will be removed by admins.
  • Restricted Blocks: No using blast-resistant deep seas blocks in building physics objects.
  • Exploit Tolerance: Zero tolerance for duping, exploiting, or utilizing X-Ray/hacked clients.

General Advice & Guidelines

  • Pre-generate chunks: We highly advise you allow your client to render the area before moving at high speeds in aircraft.
  • Seam Crossing Warning: Do not cross the seam (yellow band) in a physics object without being in at least a 3x3 cockpit. You will be unseated from a seat!
  • Optimizing contraptions: Use encased belts and shafts where possible to reduce rendering load on the server.
  • Claiming land: Ensure you claim your factory's chunks to prevent unauthorized access to your Create networks.
